Project: U.S. Grid Decarbonization Dashboard
Main question: As the US electricity mix shifted toward renewables, how did grid carbon intensity change — and which states led or lagged that transition?

Story arc:

The national grid got significantly cleaner per unit of electricity generated
That improvement was driven by coal's decline and renewables' rise
The pace of change varied dramatically by state

Three views:

View 1 — National energy mix over time: Stacked area chart, generation by source 2001–2023. Sets the "what changed" context.
View 2 — Carbon intensity vs. clean energy share: Dual-axis line chart showing grid carbon intensity declining as clean energy share rises. The "why it changed" view.
View 3 — State decarbonization map: Map of clean energy share or carbon intensity by state, filterable by year. The "where it happened" view.

Data sources: EIA electricity generation API, EPA GHG inventory
Tools: Python (data cleaning), Tableau Public (dashboard)
